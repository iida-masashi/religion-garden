# Pastor gets 15 years in prison for sexual assaults

[**Kim Seung-yeon**](https://en.yna.co.kr/reporter/index?id=37353839393837)

![Kim Seung-yeon, 김승연](images/37353839393837_113155.jpg) 

**[All News](https://en.yna.co.kr/news)**
11:24 November 22, 2018

SEOUL, Nov. 22 (Yonhap) -- A pastor was sentenced to 15 years in prison on Thursday for raping several women at his megachurch in western Seoul.

The Seoul Central District Court handed down the ruling on Rev. Lee Jae-rock of the Manmin Central Church, which has about 130,000 followers.

Lee, 75, was convicted of using his authority as he committed rapes and sexual assaults against eight female congregants of his church. He was arrested in May.
