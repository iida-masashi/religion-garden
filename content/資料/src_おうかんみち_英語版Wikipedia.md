Tenrikyo-based Japanese new religion


[![](images/250px-Okanmichi_Minato_branch_church_building.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Okanmichi_Minato_branch_church_building.jpg)

Ōkanmichi Minato branch church in Aichi Prefecture

[![](images/250px-Okanmichi_Midori_branch_church_building.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Okanmichi_Midori_branch_church_building.jpg)

Ōkanmichi Midori branch church in Aichi Prefecture

**Ōkanmichi** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language "Japanese language"): おうかんみち) is a [Tenrikyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenrikyo "Tenrikyo")-based *[shinshūkyō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_new_religions "Japanese new religions")* (Japanese new religion) that was organized by its founder, Egami Toshitane (江上寿胤), as a successor organization of [Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenri_Kami_no_Kuchiake_Basho "Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho"). It is currently based in [Nasu District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasu_District,_Tochigi "Nasu District, Tochigi"), [Tochigi Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tochigi_Prefecture "Tochigi Prefecture").

## History

Ōkanmichi was formed after a hiatus of the disbandment of the previous religious sect, *Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho*, in 1939 and upon the death of the founder of the said previous religious sect, Yamada Umejirō (山田梅次郎), in 1941. Egami Toshitane was one of Yamada Umejirō's followers and re-established Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho as a *tenkeisha* (天啓者, divine revelator), just like Yamada Umejirō, with the title, Hyōtan no Ki Kanrodai (ひょうたんの木甘露台). Egami Toshitane initially started proselytising in [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo "Tokyo") right after the end of the [Pacific War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_War "Pacific War") and eventually moved the headquarters to Nasu, Tochigi Prefecture in 1973.

Before its headquarters moved to a new location, there were numerous name changes of his organization until it became Ōkanmichi in 1960. The organization's historical name changes are listed below:

* Ichiretsukai (一列会) in 1947
* Tsukihi Kyōkai (月日教会) in 1948
* Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho Ōkanmichi Kyōkai (天理神の口明場所大還道教会) in 1955
* Tenri Kami no Kuchiake Basho Ōkanmichi Kyō (天理神の口明場所おうかんみち教) in 1957
* Ōkanmichi (おうかんみち) in 1960

Ōkanmichi means *broad path*, a term from the [Ofudesaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofudesaki "Ofudesaki") numerously mentioned by the founder of Tenrikyo, [Nakayama Miki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakayama_Miki "Nakayama Miki").

Egami Toshitane died in 1971 and was interred in Ōkanmichi Mausoleum (おうかんみち霊廟, *Ōkanmichi Reibyō*) within the headquarters.

## Successor organizations

* [Kanrodai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanrodai_(Japanese_new_religion) "Kanrodai (Japanese new religion)") (甘露台), founded by Asano Hiroshi (浅野博) in 1952
* [Kanrodai Reiri Shidōkai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanrodai_Reiri_Shidōkai "Kanrodai Reiri Shidōkai") (甘露台霊理斯道会), founded by Saitō Toshio (斎藤年男) in 1975
