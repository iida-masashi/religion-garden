From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Vegetarian restaurant in San Francisco, California, U.S.

**Greens Restaurant** is a [landmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landmark "Landmark") [vegetarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vegetarian "Vegetarian") restaurant in the [Fort Mason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Mason "Fort Mason") Center in the [Marina District, San Francisco, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_District,_San_Francisco,_California "Marina District, San Francisco, California"), overlooking the [Golden Gate Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Gate_Bridge "Golden Gate Bridge").

## History

[![](images/250px-Green_Gulch-20120325.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Green_Gulch-20120325.jpg)

[Green Gulch Farm Zen Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Gulch_Farm_Zen_Center "Green Gulch Farm Zen Center")

Founded by the [San Francisco Zen Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_Zen_Center "San Francisco Zen Center") in 1979, Greens has been credited in *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times "The New York Times")* as "the restaurant that brought vegetarian food out from sprout-infested health food stores and established it as a cuisine in America." In its early days, the restaurant's staff was composed of Zen Center students and Zen practice was incorporated into the work routines.

Greens has a legacy of female [lead chefs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chef_de_cuisine "Chef de cuisine"), beginning with founding chef Deborah Madison, and followed by Annie Somerville, Denise St. Onge, and currently Katie Reicher. The restaurant utilizes fresh produce from the organic [Green Gulch Farm Zen Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Gulch_Farm_Zen_Center "Green Gulch Farm Zen Center").
