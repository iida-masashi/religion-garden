EMAIL: sunnymall@ambikamail.com

Many Indian IT engineers settled in the area around the year 2000, drawn by its convenience and livability.

Today, the neighborhood has grown into one of Japan’s largest “Little India” communities and has become a popular cultural attraction.

Every autumn, the Diwali Festival of Lights is held, drawing crowds of all nationalities in a vibrant celebration.
