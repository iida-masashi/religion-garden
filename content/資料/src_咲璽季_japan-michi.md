## Introduction

Vegan travelers in Osaka no longer need to worry about going hungry around Tennoji and Shinsekai — this compact southern pocket of the city has quietly become one of the most rewarding plant-based dining clusters in the whole metropolis. Tennoji anchors the area with its JR hub, the soaring Abeno Harukas tower, and easy access to Nara, while Shinsekai — the retro “New World” district beneath Tsutenkaku Tower — brings a gritty, carnival energy that makes eating here feel like an event in itself. This guide covers three verified vegan and vegan-friendly restaurants across the area, from a fully plant-based kushikatsu izakaya to a beloved okonomiyaki counter, making it ideal for solo travelers, couples, and families who want to [explore more of Tennoji](https://japan-michi.com/areas/tennoji/) without compromising on their diet. For a broader look at plant-based dining across the city, check out [our Osaka vegan guide](https://japan-michi.com/vegan/) before you arrive.

## 1. Shinsekai Paprika Shokudo Vegan (新世界パプリカ食堂)

- 📍 3-4-5 Ebisu-higashi, Naniwa-ku, Osaka 556-0002 (behind Kushikatsu Daruma, Janjan Yokocho, Shinsekai)
- 🕐 12:00–14:30 / 17:30–21:00; closed Wednesdays + irregular holidays *(as of 2026-06-28)*
- 💰 ¥1,000–2,000
- 🚃 Dobutsuen-mae Station (3 min walk)
- 🌱 Vegan status: fully vegan
- 🥛 Dairy-free: Yes
- 🍷 Alcohol served: Yes
- 🗣 English menu: Yes (digital QR menu, translatable)
- 🗺 [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E6%96%B0%E4%B8%96%E7%95%8C%E3%83%91%E3%83%97%E3%83%AA%E3%82%AB%E9%A3%9F%E5%A0%82+%E6%B5%AA%E9%80%9F%E5%8C%BA+Osaka)
- 🔗 Source: [itravelforveganfood.com](https://www.itravelforveganfood.com/restaurants/shinsekai-paprika-shokudo-vegan)

**What to order:** Vegan kushikatsu skewers (¥140–370 each) — try avocado, soy meat, and vegan cheese; vegan gluten-free takoyaki (¥740)

Tucked into the back alleys of Janjan Yokocho at the foot of Tsutenkaku Tower, this fully plant-based spot reimagines Shinsekai’s most iconic street food — kushikatsu and takoyaki — without a single animal product. [The best food in Osaka](https://japan-michi.com/food/) doesn’t get more local than this: the entire menu is 100% vegan and gluten-free, making it one of the most inclusive dining options in the entire Tennoji–Shinsekai corridor.

## 2. Okonomiyaki Chitose (お好み焼き ちとせ)

- 📍 1-11-10 Taishi, Nishinari-ku, Osaka 557-0002
- 🕐 Mon, Thu–Sun 12:00–15:30 / 17:00–20:00; closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays *(as of 2026-06-28)*
- 💰 ¥800–1,200
- 🚃 Dobutsuen-mae Station (5 min walk)
- 🌱 Vegan status: vegan options available
- 🥛 Dairy-free: Yes (for the vegan okonomiyaki)
- 🍷 Alcohol served: No
- 🗣 English menu: Yes
- 🗺 [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E3%81%8A%E5%A5%BD%E3%81%BF%E7%84%BC%E3%81%8D+%E3%81%A1%E3%81%A8%E3%81%9B+%E8%A5%BF%E6%88%90%E5%8C%BA+Osaka)
- 🔗 Source: [happycow.net](https://www.happycow.net/reviews/okonomiyaki-chitose-osaka-171231)

**What to order:** Vegan okonomiyaki with soy meat (around ¥900); modan yaki with noodles

This tiny counter restaurant — just twelve seats — sits a short walk from Shinsekai in the Taishi neighbourhood and draws a loyal queue of vegan and non-vegan diners alike. The English-speaking owner takes orders while you wait outside, and the vegan okonomiyaki, made with soy meat and served without egg or bonito, is a genuinely satisfying version of Osaka’s most beloved comfort food. Arrive at opening time to avoid a long wait.

## 3. Saijiki / Vegan Taiwanese Restaurant (咲璽季)

- 📍 Tennoji Park Villa #105, 1-1-10 Sannō, Nishinari-ku, Osaka 557-0001 (adjacent to Tennoji area)
- 🕐 Please confirm current hours directly with the restaurant before visiting *(as of 2026-06-28)*
- 💰 ¥1,000–1,800
- 🚃 Tennoji Station (10 min walk)
- 🌱 Vegan status: fully vegan
- 🥛 Dairy-free: Yes
- 🍷 Alcohol served: No
- 🗣 English menu: Limited
- 🗺 [Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E5%92%B2%E7%92%BA%E5%AD%A3+%E5%A4%A9%E7%8E%8B%E5%AF%BA%E3%83%91%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AF%E3%83%B4%E3%82%A3%E3%83%A9+%E8%A5%BF%E6%88%90%E5%8C%BA+Osaka)
- 🔗 Source: [happycow.net](https://www.happycow.net/best-vegan-restaurants/osaka-japan)

**What to order:** Vegan dumplings, Taiwanese noodle soup, spring rolls

Listed among HappyCow’s top-rated vegan restaurants in Osaka, this fully plant-based Taiwanese restaurant operates out of a residential building in Sannō, a quiet neighbourhood immediately west of Tennoji Station. The kitchen avoids the five pungent spices, making it suitable for a wide range of dietary practices, and the menu of dumplings, noodles, soup, and Taiwanese fried foods offers a welcome change of pace from Japanese staples. Call or check social media ahead of your visit to confirm current opening hours, as they can change.

## Practical Info for Vegan Visitors

- 🌱 Key Japanese phrases for vegan ordering:
  - **ビーガンです** — “I am vegan”
  - **肉なしでお願いします** — “No meat, please”
  - **魚なしでお願いします** — “No fish, please”
  - **卵なしでお願いします** — “No eggs, please”
  - **乳製品なしでお願いします** — “No dairy, please”
  - **だしは何を使っていますか？** — “What broth/stock do you use?”
- 📱 Download the HappyCow app before your trip — it lets you filter by “vegan only” and shows user-verified listings with real-time reviews, which is especially useful when exploring the Tennoji and Shinsekai backstreets.
- 🛒 For vegan-safe convenience store snacks near Tennoji, look for plain onigiri with pickled plum (umeboshi) or kombu at FamilyMart and Lawson, as well as edamame, roasted nuts, and certain soy-milk drinks — always check the ingredient label for bonito (かつお) or milk (乳).
