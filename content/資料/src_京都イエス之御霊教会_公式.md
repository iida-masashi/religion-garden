- THE SUBSTANCE
- Iesu no Mitama Kyokai \[The Spirit of Jesus Church\] is not a Catholic Church but the Church which takes over the lineage from the Apostles in the Primitive (first) Church. It is really the Lord raising the Body of Christ, the True Church to be caught up, which was restored from the Apostolic Church to appear in the last days in Japan as prophesied in the Bible, having the true, perfect and high
mysteries.

- THE ORIGIN
- It is based on what the Holy Spirit was suddenly poured out in the night meeting on July 23 in Showa 8 (1933) as on the day of Pentecost (Acts 2:1-4), and since then the Holy Spirit movement began throughout the whole of Japan.

- THE BEGINNING
- It started from when the belief outlines, which Bishop Rev. Jun Murai established as on the other sheet since the name “イエス之御霊教会” (Iesu no Mitama Kyokai \[The Spirit of Jesus Church\]) was given by a heavenly vision, were officially
approved by the government on November 17 in Showa 16 (1941).

- THE DEVELOPMENT
- After the war, on March 15 in Showa 22 (1947), he leased a small house at 6-355, Ohmiyamae, Suginami-ku, Tokyo. As soon as he began to have meetings there as Tokyo Iesu no Mitama Kyokai \[The Spirit of Jesus Church in Tokyo\], many people who asked for the Holy Spirit miraculously came to church from here and there, and it spread over the whole of Japan and the Nansei (Ryukyu) Islands where the
churches were rapidly planted and have been to date,and at the present time, the churches have been also planted in the United States, Brazil, Argentina or Taiwan.
